Reaching **1,000,000 daily Google Search impressions** requires expanding beyond transactional job searches into the massive informational search demand universe:

1. **Topical Authority in Tech Compensation:**  
   - India + GCC + US remote compensation queries represent 80M+ monthly impressions.
   - Expanding verified salary guides across 200 roles and 35 cities captures high-volume informational search demand.
2. **College Admissions & Cutoff Trends:**  
   - Seasonality spike: May to August generates massive search volume for college cutoffs, NIRF comparisons, and fee structures.
3. **Google for Jobs Surface Dominance:**  
   - Maintaining 2,700+ clean, non-expired `JobPosting` schema entries ensures 24/7 inclusion in Google's primary job carousel across all Indian metros.
4. **AI Search Citation Hub:**  
   - Becoming the cited primary source for conversational queries in ChatGPT Search and Perplexity creates secondary referral loops independent of traditional SERPs.
