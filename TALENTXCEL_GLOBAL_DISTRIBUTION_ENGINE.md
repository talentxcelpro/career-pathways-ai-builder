## 2. The 4 Proven Distribution Archetypes in TalentXcel

### A. The Zapier Model: Multi-Dimensional Entity Intersections
- **Pattern:** `PERSON × ROLE × SKILL × LOCATION × EXPERIENCE × SALARY × COMPANY × CAREER PATH × RESUME × JOB`.
- **Execution:** Pages are generated only when backed by genuine inventory or calculated intelligence.
- **Example:** `/jobs/software-engineer/bangalore/python` connects live job inventory with Python skill benchmarks and Bangalore compensation percentiles.

### B. The Wise Model: Live Calculated Intelligence
- **Pattern:** The page’s value is driven by **computation and real-time data**, not 500 words of prose.
- **Execution:** Dynamic salary calculator providing 25th, 50th, 75th, and 90th percentiles, Indian New Tax Regime take-home deductions, and city purchasing power adjustments.
- **Example:** `/tools/salary-calculator?role=software-engineer&city=bangalore`.

### C. The Canva Model: Instant Actionable Tool Entrances
- **Pattern:** Search visitors looking for an actionable tool receive immediate utility before hitting a signup wall.
- **Execution:** Free ATS resume scanner provides an instant score ($78/100$) and missing keyword diagnostics before offering a 1-click optimization to save to account.
- **Example:** `/resume` (Instant ATS Parser & Keyword Recommender).

### D. The LinkedIn / Public Profile Model: Living Viral Objects
- **Pattern:** Every registered user creates a public, crawlable, verifiable professional entity (`talentxcel.in/passport/:slug`).
- **Execution:** Users share their verified Career Passport with employers, recruiters, and on LinkedIn/WhatsApp. Each shared passport attracts new visitors into the TalentXcel ecosystem.
- **Viral Math:** With $K = 0.35$–$0.42$, every 1,000 active passport holders generate 350–420 new peer acquisitions.
