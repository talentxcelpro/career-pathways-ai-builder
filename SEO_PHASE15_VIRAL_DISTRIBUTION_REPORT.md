## 3. Tiered Indexing & Crawl Budget Protection (Open-Source Best Practice)

Adopted from `programmatic-seo-engine` (2.35M pages) and `santifer-irepair` (15,500 curated pages), TalentXcel enforces strict **3-Tier Indexing**:

| Tier | Eligibility Rule | Robots Directive | Sitemap Priority | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1 (Immediate Indexation)** | High verified search demand OR substantive inventory ($\ge 10$) OR complete passport ($\ge 70\%$ completion) | `index, follow, max-snippet:-1` | **0.9** (Daily Crawl) | Capture high-intent Page 1 positions immediately |
| **Tier 2 (Standard Indexation)** | Evidenced demand, standard canonical assets, calculated tools | `index, follow` | **0.7** (Weekly Crawl) | Long-tail search expansion |
| **Tier 3 (Client-Only / Noindex)** | Sub-threshold inventory ($<3$), incomplete UGC profiles ($<50\%$), parameter spam (`?page=`, `&sort=`) | `noindex, follow` | **0.0** (Excluded) | Prevent thin-page index bloat & protect crawl budget |
