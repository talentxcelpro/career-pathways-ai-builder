## 4. Prioritized Digital PR & Authority-Building Playbook

### Channel 1: University & Campus TPO Integration (Highest Impact)
- **Opportunity:** Over 902 accredited colleges are cataloged in `colleges.talentxcel.in`.
- **Strategy:** Offer college Training & Placement Offices (TPOs) free institutional access to TalentXcel's ATS Resume Checker and Campus Hiring dashboard. In exchange, TPOs place a link under *"Recommended Student Career & Placement Tools"* on their official `.ac.in` or `.edu.in` placement portals.
- **Authority Value:** High domain rating (DR 60–85), trusted educational seed domain.

### Channel 2: B2B Directories & Verified Business Profiles
- **Opportunity:** Verified enterprise listings across Clutch, Crunchbase, G2, ProductHunt, StartupIndia, and Nasscom.
- **Strategy:** Formalize complete, branded profiles with verified address, phone, founder bio, and canonical URL `https://talentxcel.in`.
- **Authority Value:** Solidifies brand entity signals in Google's Knowledge Graph.

### Channel 3: Thought Leadership & Technical Hiring Reports
- **Opportunity:** Quarterly release of the *"TalentXcel Tech Hiring & Compensation Index"*.
- **Strategy:** Publish original data regarding fresher hiring trends, AI skill demand, and tech compensation percentiles derived from our live database of 2,711 jobs and 71 employers. Pitch findings to tech and business publications (YourStory, Inc42, Economic Times, TechCircle).
- **Authority Value:** Natural high-tier press citations and earned editorial backlinks.
