Frequently Asked Questions

How are government recruitment notifications sourced?

Notifications are compiled from official gazettes, public commission releases (.gov.in, .nic.in), and verified agency notices.

Can I filter notifications by educational qualification?

Yes. You can filter by graduate, diploma, 10th/12th pass, and specialized engineering or medical qualification criteria.

Is there any fee to access government notification details?

No. All notification summaries, eligibility criteria, and links to official commission portals are completely free.
