Frequently Asked Questions

How are salary benchmark percentiles calculated?

Benchmarks are derived from employer job compensation disclosures, verified market research datasets, and user-reported compensation data calibrated across experience tiers.

What does the What-If Scenario Simulator do?

The simulator models potential compensation trajectories based on acquiring high-demand skills, transitioning between tech stacks, or relocating between metro hubs.

How are location differentials accounted for?

Compensation figures account for cost-of-living and market demand variance across major centers like Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Mumbai, and Delhi NCR.
