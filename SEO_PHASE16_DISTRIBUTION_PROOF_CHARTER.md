## 5. Daily Scoreboard Questions (The Truth Metric)

Every day, the system evaluates:
1. **Top of Funnel:** How many new people saw TalentXcel?
2. **Channel Provenance:** How did they arrive (GSC, AI citation, WhatsApp share, LinkedIn card)?
3. **Utility Engagement:** What tool did they use (ATS scan, salary calc, passport view)?
4. **Acquisition:** How many registered?
5. **Activation:** How many completed their primary milestone?
6. **Viral Propagation:** How many shared their result, and what was the measured $K_{	ext{measured}}$?
7. **Top Growth Objects:** Which specific Career Passports and ATS scorecards drove signups?
8. **Dead Channels:** Which acquisition paths produced zero activations and should be deprioritized?
