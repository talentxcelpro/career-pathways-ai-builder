Frequently Asked Questions

What is TalentXcel?

TalentXcel is a global career intelligence platform that connects tech and leadership professionals with verified job openings, comprehensive salary percentiles, accredited college directories, and verified Career Passports.

How does the TalentScore candidate assessment work?

TalentScore evaluates candidate competencies through algorithmic assessments, Git project audits, and verified credential proofs, establishing an objective score from 0 to 100 for recruiters.

Is TalentXcel free for jobseekers?

Yes, TalentXcel offers free access to verified job searches, ATS resume compatibility scanning, salary benchmarks, and Career Passport creation for professionals worldwide.
