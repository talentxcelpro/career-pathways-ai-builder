## 2. Detailed Topic Cluster Specifications

### 2.1 Role × Metro Technology Job Clusters
- **Current Observation:** The 7-day GSC baseline shows queries like `biomedical jobs in bangalore` (Pos 5) and `clinical research jobs` (Pos 1) converting impressions into clicks immediately.
- **Search Intent:** Job seekers looking for immediate openings in specific tier-1 and tier-2 metros.
- **Content Quality Standard:**
  - Must display only live, unexpired jobs with explicit employer attribution.
  - Never generate placeholder job listings. If a city has 0 active jobs for a role, render a "No active openings today — Set an alert" state rather than empty spam pages.
- **Internal Linking:** Link from job listings to `/salary` benchmark for that role and `/resume` ATS keyword guide.

### 2.2 ATS Resume Intelligence & Role-Based Keywords
- **Search Intent:** Job seekers tailoring their CVs to pass Applicant Tracking Systems before applying.
- **High-Converting Keywords:** `ats keywords for frontend engineer`, `data analyst resume keywords`, `ats score calculator`.
- **Product Connection:**
  - Candidates paste their resume text or upload a PDF.
  - The tool calculates a real-time ATS match score against target job descriptions and highlights missing industry competencies.
  - User can save their tailored CV by claiming their **TalentXcel Career Passport**.

### 2.3 Verified Salary Intelligence Hubs
- **Search Intent:** Employed professionals researching market compensation prior to salary negotiation or job switching.
- **High-Converting Keywords:** `senior backend engineer salary range`, `compensation percentiles hyderabad`.
- **Data Integrity Protocol:**
  - Display 25th, 50th (median), and 75th percentiles derived strictly from verified candidate inputs and employer hiring bands.
  - Never fabricate or guess salaries. If data sample is low (<5 verified submissions), display an aggregated national benchmark range clearly labeled as estimated.

## 3. Anti-Spam & Programmatic Quality Gate

To protect TalentXcel from Google's helpful content systems and scaled content abuse penalties:
1. **No Thin Facet Multipliers:** Do not generate combinatoric pages like `/jobs/remote/part-time/python/bangalore/night-shift` without distinct search demand and active inventory.
2. **Canonical Deduping:** When job listings are closed or archived, retain historical content with a prominent notice or 301 redirect to the primary role hub.
3. **Value Density:** Every indexable page must contain interactive tools, real salary percentiles, verified employer contacts, or actionable career maps.
