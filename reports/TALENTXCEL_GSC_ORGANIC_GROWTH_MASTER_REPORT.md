## 11. Automated Monitoring Architecture (Phase 11)

Extended the existing Phase 1–2 Search Demand Intelligence Engine (`src/lib/seo/demandIntelligence/` + `scripts/search-intelligence/`) cleanly while keeping execution bounded, cost-efficient, and resilient to partial GSC telemetry:
- **Extended Monitoring Detector (`src/lib/seo/demandIntelligence/gscOpportunityDetector.ts` & `types.ts`):** Added codes **`J`** (`DECLINING_QUERY_ALERT`, >=30% drop vs known prior window), **`K`** (`POSITION_MOVEMENT_ALERT`, rank drop >=3 or climb <=-4 into top 15), **`L`** (`MOBILE_OPPORTUNITY`, mobile CTR gap or >1.5x desktop dominance in top 15), and **`M`** (`PAGE_GAINING_IMPRESSIONS`, page-level +35% momentum), plus enhanced **`D`** for new emerging queries (`priorImpressions === 0` when prior window is present, distinct from `null` missing-prior). Added `summarizeGscMonitoring` and strict `NaN`/`null`/missing-dimension guards.
- **Resilient Collector (`scripts/search-intelligence/sync-gsc-telemetry.ts`):**
  - Switched Google OAuth and GSC Search Analytics requests from `undici` `fetch()` to `node:https` with `Connection: close`, 30s timeouts, and exponential retry/backoff (`withGoogleRetry`).
  - Fixed an apples-to-oranges comparison bug where `28d` query impressions were previously compared against `prior7d` impressions; aligned non-overlapping `28d` vs `prior28d` windows anchored to finalized lag (`-3d`) with share-weighted prior distribution across `query+page+country+device` grains (preventing double-counting) and per-property `try/catch` isolation (`incompleteProperties`).
  - Upgraded GA4 landing attribution (`fetchGa4LandingConversions`) to aggregate both `keyEvents` and Organic Search conversion events (`application_submitted`, `guest_apply_opened`, `apply_cta_clicked`, `ats_score_generated`, `sign_up`).
- **Bounded Cycle Reporting (`scripts/search-intelligence/run-intelligence-cycle.ts`):** Fixed location fallback inventory over-attribution (`/jobs/location/*` no longer inherits national `totalActiveJobs` unless `/jobs` or `/jobs/location/india`), and surfaced `A–M` monitoring alert counts, prior-window totals, and top country metrics in daily/weekly reports.
